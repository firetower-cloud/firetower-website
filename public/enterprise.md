# Firetower Enterprise

> Your infrastructure. Our expertise.

Bring AI agent teams into your company. We help you deploy Firetower, build your first workflows, and support your rollout.

Firetower is free to self-host. Enterprise services are optional and scoped with your team. This is a services offer around the public product, not a requirement to buy a separate software edition.

## Deployment and onboarding

Set up Firetower in your environment. Connect machines and tools, define access, and get your first team ready.

- Infrastructure and access planning
- Installation and integrations
- Administrator training and handover

## Agent workflow implementation

Turn a real task into a repeatable workflow. Configure agent profiles, skills and delegation around the way your team works.

- Use-case selection and scoping
- Agent teams, profiles and skills
- Evaluation on representative tasks

## Optional ongoing support

Give administrators a direct technical contact for technical troubleshooting, upgrade and maintenance guidance, and regular deployment reviews.

Support scope, coverage hours, response targets and operating responsibilities are defined in the engagement. This page does not promise 24/7 human support or a resolution-time guarantee.

## A focused pilot

Choose two workflows and agree what success looks like before expanding. The pilot delivers:

- Firetower deployed in an agreed environment
- A first team onboarded with resource permissions configured
- Two workflows configured and evaluated
- An administrator guide and handover session
- A plan for rollout and optional support

Example workflows include issue-to-draft-pull-request, documentation maintenance, and operational reports from approved files and connected tools. These are examples, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Your infrastructure and responsibilities

Your company chooses its infrastructure, provider accounts and resource permissions. Any support access is agreed with your team. Infrastructure and AI provider costs remain separate from Firetower services. Supported provider subscriptions or API credentials remain subject to provider terms.

Bring identity, access and operational requirements to the first conversation. The team assesses supported capabilities, identifies gaps and agrees the scope before work starts. Native SSO, SCIM, certification, and other unverified capabilities are not included as advertised features of this offer.

## Engagement process

1. Define the environment, access requirements and first use cases.
2. Run the deployment and workflow pilot.
3. Train administrators and hand over operating guidance.
4. Operate independently or continue with agreed support.
