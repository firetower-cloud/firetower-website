Open a workspace to read the agent’s conversation. Use the Overview panel to follow the team working on the task.

1. Follow the lead’s conversation

Read progress, tool activity and questions. Send a message to clarify the task or change direction. Review permission requests before approving them.

2. Open a teammate or sub-workspace

In Overview → Team, click the agent you want to follow. For example, select Quota band thresholds to open the delegated review.

You now see that agent’s messages and questions. Agents in the same workspace share its files; agents with their own sub-workspace have a separate working area. The Team panel keeps the lead and other teammates within reach.

3. Return to the lead

Click the lead’s name in the Team panel, or the from link above the child conversation. Review the lead’s summary once the delegated work returns.

Continue from another device

Connect to the same Firetower installation and open the workspace. Closing a client does not stop work unless that device is also the worker. Keep the worker awake.

Change accounts

Use the workspace account control to switch accounts. See account fallback for the difference between continuing with the same agent and handing work to another agent.

Finish the work

Review outputs, ship changes or export files before ending the workspace. Stopping an agent and removing its workspace are different actions; check the confirmation before continuing.
